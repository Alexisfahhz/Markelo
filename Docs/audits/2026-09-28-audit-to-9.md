## Methodology, stated plainly

149 harness entries and 21 artboards is too many to give each an individually staged screenshot
review inside one session without it costing more than the audit is worth. What actually happened:

1. **Mechanical checks ran against 100% of source**, not a sample: `data-grid` leftover attribute,
   `@tabler` imports, raw hex outside `index.css`, raw px in inline styles, student-name/matric
   leakage in marking-facing files, AI-suggested-a-mark language, `console.error`/`debugger`
   left in source, and the border-token WCAG rule (`border-control` vs `border-default` on form
   controls). These are exhaustive, not sampled.
2. **Every one of the 149 screens was screenshotted at 1440×900** via a Puppeteer script
   (`prototype-flow/_audit_shoot5179.mjs`, deleted before commit) that clicked through the
   harness's own Next button end to end, and separately captured console errors per screen (0
   across all 149). All 21 5180 artboards were screenshotted the same way via `scrollToId`, with
   `verify.mjs` independently confirming geometry (1440×1024 on all 21) and 0 JS errors.
3. **I looked at every one of those 149+21 screenshots.** Given the volume, the write-up below
   groups screens that are visually and structurally identical (the five-state pattern —
   default/empty/loading/error/denied — repeats close to verbatim across ~30 feature areas) rather
   than giving each of 170 images its own paragraph. Screens I judged higher-risk (anything
   marking-, moderation-, or identity-facing) got individual scrutiny and are named below.
4. **The 768px tablet breakpoint was spot-checked**, not exhaustively swept, on the screens most
   likely to break it: a stat-card dashboard (Exam Officer) and the densest three-pane screen in
   the product (Marking interface). Both were broken for the same structural reason (see Finding 1),
   which is why I'm confident the fix generalizes rather than needing a screen-by-screen recheck.

All screenshots are committed at `Docs/audits/screenshots-5179/` (149 files + manifest.json) and
`Docs/audits/screenshots-5180/` (21 files + manifest.json).

## Finding 1 — severity: blocking. Tablet breakpoint was unimplemented across the entire app shell

**FIXED, re-verified.**

IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md section 3, reversed and decided 10 August 2026: the app supports desktop
*and* one tablet breakpoint, 768–1023px, with the 236px sidebar collapsing to a **72px icon rail**
— the exact reason given in the plan is that the full sidebar leaves only 468px of content at
768px, "narrower than most phones in landscape, on a product built around wide tables."

That collapse was never built. `src/ui/shell.tsx` hardcoded `w-[236px]` on both sidebar variants
(`FlatSidebar` line 247, the tree `Sidebar` line 440) with zero `max-lg:` override, while 46
`max-lg:` responsive rules already existed across 10 screen files for the *content* side of the
same breakpoint. The content-side work anticipated a sidebar that never shipped.

**Impact, screenshotted at 768×1024 before the fix:** the Exam Officer dashboard's four stat cards
were crushed into unreadable single-word-wrapped slivers ("SCRIPTS PROCES[SED]", "4,18[2]" cut
off). The Marking interface — the single highest-stakes screen in the product — had its
document viewer, thumbnail rail, and marks panel all compressed into an illegible column, with the
tab row overflowing off-screen. This is not a cosmetic issue: at the minimum supported width, the
screen markers actually enter marks on was unusable.

**Fix** (`src/ui/shell.tsx`, both `FlatSidebar` and the tree `Sidebar`, plus `TopBar`): sidebar
container gets `max-lg:w-[72px]`; the wordmark text, "WORKING AS" panel, nav labels, lock-icon
captions, and the "SIGNED IN AS" footer text all collapse behind `max-lg:hidden`, leaving centered
icons with `title=` tooltips (native, no new component) for identification; `TopBar`'s
institution/session block also gets `max-lg:hidden` since the sidebar collapse alone frees enough
width for everything else. `npx tsc --noEmit` stayed clean throughout.

**One simplification, assumed, his to override:** the tree sidebar's expandable groups
(Institution Admin's nested items) have no room for a flyout menu at 72px, so at `max-lg` the
child list stays closed regardless of click state — a comment at the `<ul>` marks this explicitly.
A real icon rail with hover flyouts is a larger, legitimate follow-up if he wants nested items
reachable at tablet width; I did not build it because choosing flyout vs. something else is a
design decision, not a mechanical fix, and it wasn't asked for.

**Re-verified after fix**, screenshots not committed (I looked, confirmed clean, moved on — this
was a spot-check, not part of the 149-screenshot set): Exam Officer dashboard now renders a proper
2×2 stat grid with full numbers ("4,182" intact); Marking interface now has a legible three-pane
layout at 768px, sidebar reduced to a clean icon column. The document-viewer/marks-panel split on
that specific screen is still tight at exactly 768px (the product's declared floor) — legible and
usable, not broken, but not spacious. I did not chase this further; it's a minor residual polish
item, not a repeat of Finding 1.

## Finding 2 — severity: minor. WCAG 2.2 SC 1.4.11 contrast failure on one checkbox

**FIXED, re-verified.**

`src/screens/settings.tsx:358` (User Settings, notification preferences) styled a checkbox with
`border-border` (`--color-border`, `#CCCCCC`, 1.61:1 against white) instead of
`border-border-control` (`#8A8A8A`, 3.45:1) — the exact failure mode
IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md section 3 names by number and calls "a real failure not a nitpick." Every
other input/select in the codebase (`src/ui/kit.tsx`) already uses the correct token; this was one
inline checkbox that didn't go through the shared component. Fixed to `border-border-control`.
`tsc --noEmit` clean.

## Findings not fixed — questions, not fixes (his call, per task order step 4)

**3. Raw pixel magic numbers with no backing token.** `style={{ minHeight: "calc(100vh - 120px)" }}`
and `style={{ maxWidth: "min(703px, 100%)" }}` appear identically across
`studentdata.tsx`, `scanning.tsx`, `admin.tsx`, and `exam.tsx` (8 occurrences, all in the same
split-pane upload/preview layout). Section 4 of the plan is explicit: *"If you need a value that
has no token, that is a signal the design system has a gap. Say so. Do not invent a one-off
value."* There is no `--reading-column-width` or `--split-pane-min-height` token, and the 120px
doesn't correspond to any measured chrome height (`TopBar` is content-sized, not fixed). I did not
invent one — the values render correctly today, this is a maintainability/DoD-letter gap, not a
visible bug, and naming a new token is exactly the kind of call the plan says is his to make.

**4. Exception Queue displays a name/matric outside the Identity Registry's dedicated flow.**
`sc-report`'s resolve screen (`triage.tsx`, "Exception queue: resolve one") shows the OCR-read name
and matric number side by side when a cover page and an extra sheet disagree, so the Exam Officer
can judge which is correct. This is Exam-Officer-only (never marker-facing, so it does not violate
rule 1 literally) and it is audit-logged with the identical mechanism as Identity Registry
("Looking up this student is recorded... written to the audit trail. The record cannot be edited or
removed."). It is coherent and compliant as built. The open question is architectural, not a
compliance failure: should every identity reveal route through one component (Identity Registry)
for a single audit surface, or is a second, purpose-built reveal inside the exception-resolution
flow the intended design? I did not touch this — changing it would be restructuring a flow, which
is out of scope for a HOW-fix.

**5. `design-system-preview` (port 5178, out of this audit's scope) still imports `@tabler/icons-react`
in its own source**, not just its `node_modules`, across 10 files. IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md section 3
already calls this app "older, separate... a component gallery, not the product. Do not build
product screens there," so I'm reporting it as the packet asked me to check, not treating it as an
audit failure of the two apps actually in scope (5179/5180 are 100% Lucide, verified by grep,
zero `@tabler` references in either).
