## MARKELO RESEARCH CONSENT FORM

**Markelo Ltd.**, Examination research study

### What this study is

We are researching how examinations are managed in Nigerian tertiary institutions. We want to understand your current process and the problems you experience. We are not selling a product to you today.

### What we ask from you

- A conversation of about 20 minutes.
- Permission to take written notes.
- Permission to photograph documents or equipment you show us (optional).
- Permission to record video or audio (optional).

### How we use your information

- We use your answers to improve the design of our product.
- We may use photographs and video clips on our website and social media pages to show our research work.
- **We will not publish your voice or your answers as quotes attached to your name** unless you tick the box below.
- We store your contact details only to follow up with you about this research.

### Your rights

- You may refuse any question.
- You may stop the conversation at any time.
- You may ask us to delete your information at any time. Contact us at the address below.
- Refusing to take part has no negative effect on you.

## Team notes, do not print this section

1. **Fill the contact details before printing.** A consent form with no contact address is not valid consent.
2. **Two copies.** Give one to the participant. Keep one.
3. **Tick boxes matter.** If the person does not tick the video box, do not record them. If they do not tick the publication box, do not put their face on Instagram.
4. **Photograph the signed form** immediately after the interview, and store it with the interview notes.
5. **The default is private.** If a box is not ticked, assume "no".
