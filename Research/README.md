# Markelo Research Kit

**Created:** 21 July 2026
**Purpose:** Turn the founder's field research campaign into usable evidence for product design and for funding applications.

---

## The problem this kit solves

The team has spoken to approximately four people. The product has 22 user stories and six roles. Almost every design decision is currently a guess.

The founder's research campaign is the chance to fix this. But a campaign run by many untrained people, with no shared instrument, produces anecdotes, not evidence. This kit makes the results comparable and usable.

## How to run the campaign

**Before anyone goes out**
1. A lawyer reviews the consent form. Add the contact email and phone. Print two copies per participant.
2. Everyone reads `02-discussion-guides.md`. The rules at the top matter more than the questions.
3. Everyone receives a copy of the capture sheet from `04-capture-and-synthesis.md`.

**During an interview**
1. Get the consent form signed first.
2. Do not describe Markelo. Ask about their current process.
3. Ask to see and photograph artifacts. Paper is stronger evidence than opinion.
4. Record their exact words, not your summary.

**After an interview**
1. Fill the capture sheet within one hour.
2. Mark which assumptions were supported, contradicted, or still unknown.
3. Photograph the signed consent form and store it with the notes.

**Every week**
1. Count the assumption results across all interviews.
2. Apply the evidence rule: one source is a signal, three agreeing sources are evidence, any contradiction stops the design.
3. Update the assumption map.
4. Write a three-line summary: what we learned, what it changes, what we still cannot answer.

## The five questions that matter most

If a conversation is cut short, get these:

1. **Walk me through one full examination, start to finish.** (Tests A1)
2. **What scanning equipment does this office have? May I see it?** (Tests A2)
3. **Did a script go missing in the last two exam periods? What happened?** (Tests A5)
4. **Where do you upload final results? What file format does it accept?** (Tests A4)
5. **Who approves buying new software here?** (Tests A11, the biggest current gap)
