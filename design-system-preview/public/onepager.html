The week

Five days, one component library

MON20 Jul

Foundations

Figma Variables (raw palette → named tokens), the type scale, layout grid, interaction states, and the accessibility focus ring. No components until this is right.

TUE21 Jul

Atoms

Buttons, status badges, inputs, dropdowns, and file upload, every one with all of its states built in, not bolted on.

WED22 Jul

Core molecules

The data table (half the app), role-aware navigation, and feedback, alerts, toasts, and the offline indicator that marking depends on.

THU23 Jul

Overlays & states

Modals, side panels, steppers, progress, and the three states every screen needs, empty, error, and access-denied.

FRI24 Jul

Composites, docs & review

The app-specific pieces, script viewer, mark entry, moderation compare, then usage docs, a full team review, and tag v1.0.

How we work

Three build, ten stay in sync

01

A small build team

Three designers build the core. Everyone else places components rather than redrawing them, which is the whole point.

02

Async by default

A five-minute recording each evening and a running decisions log. No one has to watch us work live to stay current.

03

Training inside the work

The three designers still growing in Figma rebuild one component a day. Upskilling happens on the real thing, at no extra cost.

By Friday

A versioned v1.0 system, and a team that's unblocked

Every app component built once, documented, and consistent. From Monday the 27th, all 16+ screens get designed fast and on-system, no more one-off buttons, no more consistency debt.
