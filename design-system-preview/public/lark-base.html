The five squares

Each record shows five squares in this order. Green = built. A screen is only “Ready for dev” when all five are green, US v2 added the Denied (403) requirement to every role-gated story.

 Empty, nothing to show yet, with a way to populate
 Loading, skeleton matching the final shape
 Error, something failed, marking is safe
 Success, the happy path
 Denied, 403, role not allowed (identity is officer-only)
